# Executable Worlds

[Review map](README.md) · [Main survey](../../README.md#paradigms)

Programs and simulators connect inferred state, transitions, execution, and rendering. **Direct video systems**, **visual bridges**, and **planning foundations** are separated in the scope column. Scene reconstruction, program execution, and physical identification are distinct claims; see the [case study](../world-models.md).
